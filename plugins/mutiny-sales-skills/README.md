# Sales Skills by Mutiny

Free sales skills for Claude, from the team at [Mutiny](https://www.mutinyhq.com). They turn Claude into a sales assistant that sounds like you: follow-ups, meeting prep, a daily brief and check-ins in your voice, plus customer-ready pricing proposals, business cases and deal rooms built from your calls.

## Use it

Start with setup: say "Build my sales profile" and Claude builds your sales profile from the tools you've connected. Then ask for what you need, like "Run my Mutiny follow-up" or "Build the Mutiny pricing proposal for [company]". Each skill also runs as /mutiny-sales-skills:<skill-name>.

## Data

The skills are instructions only. The plugin bundles no connectors and runs no code, and it sends nothing to Mutiny.

When you run a skill, Claude reads deal data, including names and email addresses, from the connectors you've already added yourself, such as your CRM, email, calendar, call recorder, and Slack. Some skills also use Claude's web search to look up public information, like a meeting attendee's role or your own company's website. If you ask, Claude can send a skill's output to you through your own connected tools.

The setup skill saves your sales profile as files in your own Claude project, which you control. Nothing is stored outside your Claude account.

More at [github.com/MutinyGTM/mutiny-sales-skills](https://github.com/MutinyGTM/mutiny-sales-skills).
